How material is managed

Openness with appropriate safeguards

The website is an early community publication. Its governance approach is intended to protect contributors, distinguish proposals from commitments and make public material accountable.

Status of SIDM

SIDM emerged from steering-group activity associated with the BCS Faculty of Health and Care and broader engagement with other standards communities. The arrangements for formal membership, representation, editorial approval and external affiliations are developing. This website does not purport to announce a new legal entity or claim endorsement from any external body.

Types of material

Public information: purpose, scope and signposts intended to explain SIDM. Proposals: ideas open for consideration, not decisions to fund or initiate work. Community contributions: material authored by contributors, reflecting their views unless explicitly adopted. Approved publications: material that has passed an agreed review and release process. No document should be described as approved without evidence of that decision.

Publication and review

Working notes, internal meeting records and draft contributions should not be published by default. Publication should follow an appropriate check for accuracy, confidentiality, intellectual property, quality and the authority to speak on behalf of any organisation. The website source repository can preserve review and change history through pull requests.

Institutional statements and attribution

Views on these pages are not automatically the views of BCS, HL7 UK, PRSB, NHS organisations or other bodies with which participants may be associated. Statements or consultation responses in another organisation's name require that organisation's explicit clearance and its own publication procedure.

Use of AI tools

Contributors should disclose material AI assistance in substantive publications where relevant, distinguish generated analysis from independently verified claims and take responsibility for the accuracy of published content. Confidential information must not be entered into tools or public repositories without appropriate authority.

Licensing and reuse

Website code may be reused under the licence published in its GitHub repository. Written contributions and third-party materials retain their original rights unless a separate licence or permission is stated. Do not assume that linking to a standards document grants the right to redistribute its content.

Corrections and enquiries

Report a factual correction or propose an improvement through the website issue tracker. For a new community question, use the community issue tracker.
