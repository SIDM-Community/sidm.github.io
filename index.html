What the community can do

Learn, connect and contribute

SIDM's early work is concerned with bridging technical communities and those who work with standards in health and care.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Make technical subjects accessible

Curate introductions, explanations and routes into useful standards material instead of assuming everyone starts as an expert.

02 / DISCUSS

Bring different perspectives together

Connect computing professionals, clinicians, data specialists, standards developers, managers and others affected by digital health.

03 / IMPROVE

Explore practical problems

Identify gaps between specifications and implementation, and discuss approaches that can be evaluated and reused.

04 / SHARE

Publish responsibly

Develop open material with clear provenance, review and attribution. Distinguish community discussion from formally endorsed positions.
